Gamcheon: quick planning notes

Expect slopes, steps, narrow alleys and popular photo viewpoints. This is a residential neighborhood: stay out of private doorways, take litter home and be considerate with photos.

Read the Visit Busan official tourism site for current access and transport guidance.

Blueline Park: quick planning notes

The Beach Train and Sky Capsule are separate products. Choose based on your preferred route, travel pace and ticket availability. Prices and timetables can change.

Check the Haeundae Blueline Park official website before booking.
